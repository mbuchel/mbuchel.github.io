sass2mlir / Manual

Recompiling FlashAttention

Follow the actual source-program pass from admitted FA2 arithmetic through overlap, work mapping, machine lowering and checked publication.

Case study · H100

Recover FA2, then construct a fresh Hopper program

flash-attention-resynth normalizes an admitted FA2 function, recovers its arithmetic, constructs a complete logical attention program, transforms its loop and work mapping, and lowers a fresh selected kernel body. The current normalization uses independently constructed instruction forms. The pinned FA3 file supplies the publication ABI container; its selected instruction body is replaced completely.

The built-in example includes the exact noncausal input and output from the accepted October 14 study. No upload is needed. Its comparison uses the experimental browser lifter to inspect the two binaries. The native compiler IR and generation receipt are separate artifacts from the actual native pass. The browser does not execute the native transformation or GPU code.

Admission

Arithmetic witnesses and reviewed program facts

recover_flash_attention checks the complete instructions and metadata of two reviewed FP16 D64 SM90 source programs, allowing supported parameter relocation. The reference must match the full pinned SM90a ABI-image hash. An attention-like name or compatible-looking function signature is insufficient.

The HGMMA normalization stage constructs its own instruction forms, then attention_semantic_recover follows actual SSA dependencies for matrix endpoints and online maximum, exponentiation, denominator, FP16 probability packing and output rescaling. Changed or unexplained arithmetic refuses. Mask, iteration and epilogue construction additionally depend on the admitted reviewed program profile. The IR explicitly labels that boundary reviewed-program-profile-not-dynamic-ssa-proof.

Recovery is tied to the reviewed source program and its recorded numerical policy. Retiling changes rounding and reduction structure. FTZ, approximate exponent/reciprocal behavior, FMA and empty-state handling remain explicit obligations; the browser’s structural comparison does not establish numerical equivalence or a broader runtime contract.

The pass

Follow the code from equations to an image

The orchestration is mlrw_attention_program in dialect/lib/SassPasses.cpp. The implementations below live in passes/flash-attn-resynth/mlir/:

StageImplementationResult and contract
Recoverattention_semantic.cpp, attention_semantic_ir.cppBind source arithmetic to SSA witnesses and express the recovered equations in vector/arith/math IR.
Constructattention_program.cppBuild Q/K/V reads, descending key tiles, masking, online softmax, PV and O/LSE stores over fresh tensor arguments.
Overlapattention_program_overlap.cppRewrite the loop into a prologue, carried state and final drain: current QK precedes previous PV, which finishes before the current online update rescales its output.
Map workattention_program_schedule.cppConstruct persistent ticket iteration and plan three M64 math groups, one producer group, two K stages and two V stages.
Lowerattention_program_lower.cpp and the producer/consumer/transfer loweringsEmit physical TMA, HGMMA, scalar fragments, addresses, barriers and epilogue.
Schedule and allocateattention_program_controls.cpp, attention_program_allocate.cppAssign ordinary completion controls and registers; check complete matrix groups, physical conflicts and outstanding accesses.
Publishattention_program_publish.cpp, attention_program_image.cppRecheck the physical protocol, finalize a selected function, require every word to be regenerated and rebuild its ELF resources/metadata in the pinned ABI container.

A good pass keeps these contracts separate. Logical QK/PV overlap does not prove that emitted matrix operations complete before register or shared-memory reuse. Persistent work assignment must cover each tile exactly once and keep producer/consumer tickets consistent. TMA needs correct descriptors, transaction sizes, stage generations and releases. Ordinary scoreboard waits, asynchronous matrix completion and shared-stage ownership each require their own checks. Structural changes invalidate allocation and scheduling; the lowering and publication paths rerun the required checks before emitting an image.

Read the rewrite

Instruction normalization is followed by whole-body regeneration

The early normalizer converts the original N128 matrix windows: QK uses four HGMMA.64x128x16.F32 operations per M64 source half; PV uses eight HGMMA.64x64x16.F32 operations per M64 source half for the N128 reduction (two M64 halves in the source window). It constructs these forms independently and refuses if any source HMMA remains.

The final noncausal program then uses N192. One logical M64×N192×D64 QK contraction needs four K16 issues; one M64×N192 by N192×D64 PV contraction needs twelve. Thread roles, descriptors, registers and instruction addresses change with that reconstruction. The following are actual representative regions of the two bundled functions, not replacements at matching PCs:

// Original FA2 matrix region
/*1180*/ LDSM.16.M88.4 R12, [R3+0x4000];
/*11a0*/ LDSM.16.M88.4 R4, [R234+0x2000];
/*1230*/ HMMA.16816.F32 R56, R4.reuse, R12, RZ;

// Fresh program: one complete QK contraction
/*0b20*/ WARPGROUP.ARRIVE;
/*0b30*/ HGMMA.64x192x16.F32 R24, gdesc[UR32], RZ, !UPT;
/*0b40*/ HGMMA.64x192x16.F32 R24, gdesc[UR36], R24;
/*0b50*/ HGMMA.64x192x16.F32 R24, gdesc[UR40], R24;
/*0b60*/ HGMMA.64x192x16.F32 R24, gdesc[UR44], R24, gsb0;
/*0b70*/ WARPGROUP.DEPBAR.LE gsb0, 0x0;

The instruction excerpts also include source transport, the generated TMA transaction, probability packing/publication and the recurrent PV sequence, with exact function PCs.

Online state

Why previous PV must finish before the current rescale

For processing step t, use A for the unnormalized output and m for the unscaled row maximum. Actual key-tile indices descend. The base-two scale is λ = fp32(0.125 × log2(e)):

S_t = Q × K_tᵀ                      // masked invalid scores → −∞
m_t = max(m_previous, rowmax(S_t))
p_t = 2^(S_t × λ − m_t × λ)
α_t = 2^((m_previous − m_t) × λ)
l_t = α_t × l_previous + rowsum(p_t)
P_t = fp16(p_t)
A_t = α_t × A_previous + P_t × V_t

The denominator reduces the FP32 weights; PV consumes their FP16 rounding. The readable equations omit explicit FTZ/FMA and empty-state guards that are retained in the emitted IR. The final epilogue uses a guarded reciprocal for O and computes LSE from the unscaled maximum and denominator.

The transformed carry is (m, l, B, P_pending, V_pending), where B_t = α_t × A_previous is the output seed before tile t is accumulated. QK for the next tile does not consume that seed, so it can run first. Previous PV then completes A_previous; the current online update must use that completed result before forming its next rescaled seed. A final PV outside the loop drains the last pending tile, even if the recurrent loop has no iterations.

The decisive bundled IR lines are 629 (current QK), 646 (previous PV), and 647 (the recurrent online call consuming the result of line 646). Lines 671 and 673 show the five-value carry and final drain. This is a dependency rewrite of a logical program, not a proof that a particular hardware wait can be removed.

Physical protocol

Data arrival, matrix completion and ownership are separate

The CTA reserves four 128-thread warpgroups. TIDs 0–31 execute the producer; TIDs 32–127 exit after register deallocation. TIDs 128–511 are three math groups, each covering 64 rows. The noncausal work ticket starts at CTAID.X, advances by grid.x, and decodes into batch, head and one M192 query tile. The K/V slot cursor and stage generations continue across query tickets. They are different state from that work ticket.

Each K/V stage has a ready barrier initialized to count 1 and an empty barrier initialized to count 3. The producer waits for ownership, declares exactly 0x6000 (24,576) expected bytes and issues UTMALDG.4D. Consumers must establish the matching ready epoch before reading. One leader per math group releases the stage only after its relevant HGMMA reads retire.

In the recurrent overlap path, the current QK batch is older than previous PV. A gsb0, 0x1 wait can retire QK and expose an independent maximum prefix while PV remains in flight. A gsb0, 0x0 wait then retires PV before its result is consumed, V is released or shared P is overwritten. Compact wait cleanup needs the matching CFG proof. Ordinary scoreboard controls cannot prove those matrix and ownership obligations.

Q has its own handoff: named barrier 10 with 416 participants (384 math + 32 producer threads). Math hands Q back after its final QK; final PV and output use separate P/V/O storage. Applying the K/V empty-barrier cycle to this Q path would describe the wrong program.

Compiler interface

Inspect the program, then publish the image

Choose one admitted source section. SOURCE_SYMBOL is its full symbol from inspect; fa3.cubin stands for the pinned ABI container.

build/sass-mlir inspect original.cubin
build/sass-mlir optimize original.cubin \
  --passes flash-attention-program --reference fa3.cubin \
  --kernel SOURCE_SYMBOL --out attention.mlir
build/sass-mlir research parse-check attention.mlir

flash-attention-program runs the source-program construction through physical lowering. Its diagnostic module retains logical functions and unfinished publication markers. It has no launch contract; lower refuses to treat it as a runnable kernel. Publication uses the exclusive image pass:

build/sass-mlir optimize original.cubin \
  --passes flash-attention-resynth --reference fa3.cubin \
  --kernel SOURCE_SYMBOL --out converted.cubin

The interface alone does not reproduce an experimental schedule. The bundled receipt pins the measured compiler and input/output hashes, and records completion=dependencies, pipeline=overlap, transport=prefetch, mapping=persistent, scalar=batched, mask=columns and mma_waits=compact policies. The published image is accompanied by converted.cubin.launch.json.

Three routes with different meanings

flash-attention (alias pc) is the separate producer/consumer prologue rewrite, which retains the source consumer arithmetic and subsequent copy loop. flash-attention-target prepares an unchanged FA3 reference control. flash-attention-resynth constructs and publishes the fresh source program shown here. Their artifacts and performance claims cannot be interchanged.

Launch

The sidecar is part of the result

The hash-bound sidecar describes lowering=source-program, both image hashes, typed parameters, work mapping, 512-thread blocks and mask-specific shared memory. The supported adapter prepares tensor maps and parameter bindings.

These are the publication contracts for the measured images; resource sizes belong to these exact images:

The bundled noncausal source has 3,112 instruction words. Its output has 1,929 fresh words and a 2,944-byte target parameter layout. The full cubins contain other functions; the example selects only the exact symbols in its manifest.

The causal counter reset belongs inside a captured complete invocation too. Replacing a cubin alone cannot adapt FA2’s parameter buffer, block size or shared-memory request. Use the supported adapter and its exact bound sidecar.

Download all 108 timing cells, including unrounded means and each process mean in milliseconds. The table above converts those values to microseconds.

Across equally weighted cells the geometric means are 1.220× FA2 throughput and 97.32% of FA3 throughput: 18.06% lower latency than FA2 and 2.75% higher than FA3. 32 cells are slower than FA2; 90 are slower than FA3. Native and graph measurements remain separate; these are descriptive ratios, not statistical-significance claims.

The retained verification accepts 432 numerical validations and 1,296 gates across ordinary execution, memcheck and synccheck. This covers recorded inputs, not every finite FP16 value; finite extreme-input counterexamples remain. Full Nsight profiling is not accepted: a causal B1/H8/S4096/D64 application passed replay 1 numerics but raised CUDA_ERROR_ILLEGAL_INSTRUCTION on replay 2. Only 25 of 162 captures completed; the cause remains unresolved. Accepted ordinary timing and sanitizer evidence does not resolve that profiling fault.

The October 14 full measurement record and corresponding paper evidence retain the complete case data and audit witnesses. The earlier study and older donor or retained-scaffold results remain historical evidence for their own images. These results do not validate arbitrary current builds or other fixtures.
